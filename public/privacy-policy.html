Privacy Policy for CheckMate

Effective Date: September 14, 2026

1. Information We Do Not Collect

CheckMate is a local-first Progressive Web App (PWA). We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any of your personal data, job records, photos, or materials to any external servers.

2. Local Storage

All data entered into CheckMate—including jobs, equipment, materials, and photos—is stored exclusively and securely on your device's local storage within your browser. If you clear your browser's data or uninstall the application without exporting a backup, your data will be permanently lost.

3. Camera and Photo Permissions

CheckMate requests access to your device's camera and photo gallery solely for the purpose of attaching before/after photos and receipts to your local job records. These images never leave your device.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the app does not collect user data or have a server backend, any changes will only reflect updates to app functionality. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact the developer.
